November 2026

VuFind® Hamburg 2026

Hamburg, Germany — November 16–17, 2026

As of August 6, 2026 the call for contributions is open.

The annual VuFind® German users' meetup “VuFind Hamburg 2026” will be held in Hamburg, Germany, on the 16th and 17th of November 2026 at the State and University Library Hamburg. The event will be two half-days with workshops on the first and presentations on the second day. The second day will be a hybrid event which will allow virtual participation by both presenters and attendees. We aim to organize the workshops on the first day to be virtual or hybrid as well, but are still investigating whether we can make this possible.

For more information, feel free to contact the organizing team at discovery@sub.uni-hamburg.de.
